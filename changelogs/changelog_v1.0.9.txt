FeePal v1.0.9 Changelog
=======================
Release Date: May 15, 2026

[Reminder]
- Added selection menu in fee reminder screen in Admin Portal.
- Admins can now send reminders to all parents or select specific parents.

[Fee Alert]
- Revamped Fee Alert message system.
- In-app notifications and emails now provide more detailed information regarding student name and specific dues.

[Due Date]
- Fixed a bug in the due date selector.
- Past dates are now strictly prohibited from selection to ensure data integrity.

[Fee Alert Tab]
- Added a dedicated Fee Alert tab for parents.
- Parents can now see historical logs of when fees were created and issued by the school administration.

[SMS Integration]
- SMS Integration is now fully operational.
- Smart routing: Parents with SMS Alerts preference will receive only SMS notifications to avoid redundancy.

[Email Integration]
- Email alerts system optimized.
- Automated emails are sent specifically to parents who have enabled Email Alerts in their preferences.

[UI Refinements]
- Parent Dashboard: The student fee summary card now displays "Annual Year Status" for a more comprehensive overview.
- Fixed layout issues and improved localization for both English and Urdu.
