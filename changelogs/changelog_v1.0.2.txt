FeePal Changelog - v1.0.2
==========================
Release Date: 2026-05-05

[Security & Authentication]
- IMPLEMENTED: Strict unique constraints for School Address and Admin Email during registration.
- IMPLEMENTED: Secure 6-digit Email OTP Verification flow for all new Administrator accounts.
- ENHANCED: Atomic signup rollback mechanism to prevent partial account creation on failures.

[Notifications & Alerts]
- IMPLEMENTED: Intelligent Alerting Engine for automated parent notifications.
- FIXED: Real-time synchronization of in-app alerts when fee vouchers are issued.
- ENHANCED: Professional multi-channel (Push & In-App) notification payloads for fee alerts.

[Data Integrity & UX]
- ENHANCED: Mandatory field validation with visual indicators (asterisks and red backgrounds) on Signup and Student Management modules.
- UPDATED: Standardized class selections to Primary levels (Class 1-10) for simplified management.
- FIXED: Real-time phone number formatting (+92 3XX XXXXXXX) across all administrative forms.

[Bug Fixes]
- Resolved minor UI inconsistencies in the Student Management sheet.
- Fixed session restoration issues for multi-child parent accounts.
- Optimized database queries for faster dashboard loading.
