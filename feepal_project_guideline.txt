================================================================================
                               FEEPAL PROJECT GUIDELINE
================================================================================

This document provides a detailed explanation of the FeePal project, its 
architecture, and its features in simple English for non-technical people.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE TECHNOLOGIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IS FLUTTER?
Flutter is a tool created by Google that allows developers to build beautiful 
apps for Android and iPhones using a single set of code. Instead of writing 
two different apps for different phones, we write one app that works on both.

WHAT IS DART?
Dart is the programming language used by Flutter. Think of Flutter as the 
building materials (bricks, windows, paint) and Dart as the instructions 
on how to put them together.

HOW FLUTTER WORKS:
Flutter works like a professional painter. It "paints" every button, text, 
and image on the screen very quickly (60 times per second), making the app 
feel very smooth and fast.

WHAT IS FIREBASE?
Firebase is a "Backend as a Service" from Google. Imagine a house where 
Flutter is the beautiful interior and exterior design, and Firebase is the 
foundation, plumbing, and electricity that runs behind the walls.

WHY FIREBASE IS THE BEST OPTION:
1. Speed: It allows us to build features very quickly.
2. Real-time: Changes show up instantly across all devices.
3. Security: It has built-in systems to keep user data safe.
4. Scale: It can handle 10 users or 10 million users without crashing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA AND DATABASE (FIRESTORE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IS FIRESTORE?
Firestore is the database where we store all our project information (student 
names, fee records, school details).

COLLECTIONS AND DOCUMENTS:
- Collections: Think of these as folders in a filing cabinet (e.g., "Students").
- Documents: These are the individual files inside the folder (e.g., "John Doe's 
  Info"). Each document contains specific details like name, roll number, 
  and class.

FIRESTORE RULES:
These are the "security guards" of our data. They ensure that a parent can 
only see their own child's data and not someone else's, and that only 
authorized school admins can change fee records.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PUSH NOTIFICATIONS (FCM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IS FCM?
FCM stands for Firebase Cloud Messaging. It is the postman that delivers 
messages (notifications) from our system to the user's phone.

HOW IT WORKS:
When an admin sends a fee reminder, the app tells FCM to deliver a message 
to a specific phone.

FCM TOKENS:
An FCM Token is like a unique digital address for each phone. When you install 
the app, your phone gets a "token" and gives it to our database. When we want 
to send you a notification, we tell FCM to send it to your specific token.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. KEY PROJECT FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FEE MANAGEMENT & STRUCTURE:
Admins create a "Fee Template" for each class (e.g., Class 5 monthly fee = 5000). 
The system then automatically generates a "Voucher" for every student in 
that class. This saves the school from manually creating thousands of records.

CORE RELATIONSHIPS:
- Super Admins: The owners of the FeePal platform who manage the schools.
- Admins (Schools): They manage their own students, and fees.
- Students: They belong to a school and are linked to their parents.
- Parents: They use the app to track their children's fees and pay them.

AI OCR VERIFICATION (GOOGLE ML KIT):
When a parent pays a fee and uploads a photo of the receipt (voucher proof), 
the app uses Google ML Kit (an Artificial Intelligence tool). It "reads" the 
text on the receipt to verify the amount and date automatically. This prevents 
fake receipts and saves the admin from manual checking.

CLOUDINARY:
Cloudinary is a service we use to store and manage images. When a school 
uploads a logo or a parent uploads a receipt, it is stored securely on 
Cloudinary's servers and served to the app whenever needed.

SMS GATEWAY:
For parents who might not have internet or the app installed, admins can 
subscribe to SMS plans. The system uses a local SMS Gateway API to send 
automated text messages directly to the parent's phone number as a reminder.

SUBSCRIPTION PLANS:
Schools (Admins) choose between Monthly or Yearly plans to use FeePal. 
They pay the FeePal team, upload the proof of payment, and once the 
Super Admin verifies it, their account is activated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PROJECT PURPOSE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FeePal is a fee reminder and payment tracking application designed to bridge 
the gap between schools and parents. Its primary goals are:
1. To automate the tedious process of fee generation.
2. To ensure parents never miss a payment through smart reminders.
3. To provide a transparent and secure way to track all financial transactions.
4. To simplify the verification of payments using modern AI technology.

So far, we have built a robust system that handles multi-role logins, 
automated voucher generation, real-time notifications, sibling management 
(parents seeing all their kids in one place), and a secure subscription system 
for schools.
